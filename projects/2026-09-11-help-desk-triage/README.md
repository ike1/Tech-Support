# Help-desk triage and SLA workflow

**Week of 2026-09-11**  
Design a service-desk workflow that routes incidents consistently and protects response-time commitments.

## Scenario

You support a 150-user organization with business-hours support and four priority levels.

## Objective

Practice a small, realistic support workflow that can be completed, reviewed, and improved in one week.

## Checklist

- [ ] Map five common request types to an ITIL-style incident, service request, or access request category.
- [ ] Define P1-P4 impact/urgency rules, response targets, escalation owners, and an SLA-breach warning.
- [ ] Create three sanitized sample tickets with assignment group, customer updates, and closure codes.
- [ ] Review the workflow against a weekly ticket report and record one improvement.

## Deliverable

A triage decision tree, SLA matrix, and three sample tickets.


## Success measure

At least 90% of sample tickets are categorized and assigned correctly on first review.

## Notes

- Do not include passwords, personal data, or production secrets in this project.
