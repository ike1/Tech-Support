# Endpoint inventory and lifecycle audit

**Week of 2026-09-26**  
Reconcile endpoint records with an asset register and identify lifecycle, patching, and ownership gaps.

## Scenario

You administer laptops through an MDM platform and need evidence for an internal control review.

## Objective

Practice a small, realistic support workflow that can be completed, reviewed, and improved in one week.

## Checklist

- [ ] Define required CMDB fields: asset tag, serial number, owner, cost center, OS version, encryption, and lifecycle state.
- [ ] Reconcile ten sanitized records against MDM data and classify discrepancies by root cause.
- [ ] Document joiner, mover, leaver, lost-device, and secure-retirement handoffs.
- [ ] Prioritize remediation using risk, age, warranty status, and patch compliance.

## Deliverable

A reconciliation report, lifecycle RACI, and audit-ready checklist.


## Success measure

100% of sample assets have an owner and lifecycle state; all exceptions have an assigned action.

## Notes

- Do not include passwords, personal data, or production secrets in this project.
