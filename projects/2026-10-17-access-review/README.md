# Quarterly access-review remediation

**Week of 2026-10-17**  
Review privileged and leaver access, document approvals, and close exceptions with evidence.

## Scenario

You are preparing evidence for a quarterly least-privilege and joiner/mover/leaver control.

## Objective

Practice a small, realistic support workflow that can be completed, reviewed, and improved in one week.

## Checklist

- [ ] Export a sanitized sample of application and group memberships with manager and system-owner fields.
- [ ] Identify dormant accounts, excessive privilege, and leaver access; do not disable production users in this exercise.
- [ ] Route exceptions for owner approval and record due dates, compensating controls, and closure evidence.
- [ ] Summarize completion rate and overdue risk for the service owner.

## Deliverable

An access-review worksheet, exception register, and owner-ready summary.


## Success measure

100% of sampled privileged accounts have a current owner decision and evidence link.

## Notes

- Do not include passwords, personal data, or production secrets in this project.
